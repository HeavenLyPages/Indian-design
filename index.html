Places with stories in their stone

A little walk
through history.

From an ancient Buddhist stupa to the palaces of Rajasthan, India's past is still part of the everyday landscape. Here are a few places we love to explore slowly.

3rd century BCE · Madhya Pradesh

Great Stupa, Sanchi

Commissioned under Emperor Ashoka, its stone gateways tell stories of the Buddha and the ancient routes of India.

Explore central India ↗

From 1199 · Delhi

Qutub Minar

Begun by Qutb al-Din Aibak and continued by his successors, the minar rises through centuries of Delhi Sultanate history.

Explore Delhi & Agra ↗

1632–1653 · Agra

The Taj Mahal

Commissioned by Shah Jahan and built in white marble, the mausoleum is framed by gardens and four minarets.

Explore Agra ↗

1799 · Jaipur

Hawa Mahal

Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh commissioned the Palace of Winds, its honeycomb façade made for air and observation.
